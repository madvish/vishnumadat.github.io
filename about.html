The Story Behind
the Research

Equity research, derivatives, FX markets — and a relentless drive to understand what moves capital.

Driven by Markets,
Grounded in Fundamentals

I'm Vishnu Madat, a CFA Level III Candidate and Research Associate at Greenhill Consulting LLP. With a strong foundation in equity research, derivatives, and FX markets, I bring an economist's perspective to analysing financial markets.

My work involves conducting end-to-end fundamental research on sector-agnostic investment ideas — combining industry analysis, business-quality assessment, competitive positioning and risk analysis to evaluate investment opportunities. I build DCF models and valuation frameworks, synthesise earnings calls and regulatory filings, and translate complex macro and market data into actionable investment views.

I cleared both CFA Level I and Level II in my first attempts, with a Level II score of 2725 on a 2000–2900 scale. Alongside my CFA journey, I hold the NISM Series-XV (Research Analyst) certification and graduated with a 9.4 CGPA in BMS Finance from S.I.E.S College, Mumbai.
