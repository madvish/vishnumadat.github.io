Complete disclaimer, risk disclosure, and regulatory information for the virtual model equity portfolio.

1 Purpose of the Model Portfolio

This website displays a hypothetical ("model" or "virtual") equity portfolio maintained by Vishnu Madat for educational and investment-research demonstration purposes. The portfolio is designed to illustrate the application of fundamental equity research, sector allocation, and portfolio construction principles.

The portfolio is not operated as a fund, managed account, advisory service, or investment scheme of any kind. No money is being pooled, managed, or invested on behalf of any visitor.

2 Hypothetical & Simulated Nature

The portfolio may contain simulated holdings, transactions, portfolio weights, and performance figures. These do not necessarily represent actual investments or achievable investment returns. The "trades" displayed are theoretical and were not necessarily executed in any real brokerage account.

Important: Hypothetical performance results have many inherent limitations. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profits or losses similar to those shown. There are frequently sharp differences between hypothetical performance and actual results subsequently achieved.

3 Performance Limitations

Performance figures shown are price returns calculated from closing prices since the portfolio inception date. They exclude dividends, transaction costs, taxes, slippage, and other expenses that would reduce actual returns.

Hypothetical and historical performance have inherent limitations and do not guarantee future results. Past performance — whether actual or hypothetical — is not indicative of future performance.

4 Benchmark Methodology

The portfolio is benchmarked against the Nifty 500 Total Return Index (or Nifty 500 price index, as noted in the data). Alpha is calculated as portfolio return minus benchmark return over the same period. This is a simple arithmetic difference, not a risk-adjusted measure.

The benchmark is used for illustrative comparison only. The portfolio's composition, concentration, and sector weights differ materially from the benchmark index.

5 Risk Disclosure

Equity investments involve market risk, liquidity risk, valuation risk, concentration risk, sector risk, and other risks, including the possible loss of capital. The value of investments can go down as well as up. Concentrated portfolios (fewer holdings) may be subject to greater volatility than diversified portfolios.

Past or hypothetical performance does not guarantee future results. Investors should be aware that all investments carry risk and should not invest money they cannot afford to lose.

6 Data Limitations

Portfolio data — including holdings, prices, returns, and sector weights — is updated manually and may not reflect the most current market prices. Data is sourced from publicly available information and may contain errors or omissions.

No warranty is made as to the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any data presented on this website.
